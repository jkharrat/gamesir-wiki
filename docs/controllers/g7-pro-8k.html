Known issues and fixes

Documented problems with documented solutions. Each entry cites where the fix comes from.

Controller never appears in GameSir Nexus
Likely causeThe G7 Pro 8K PC is not a Nexus device. GameSir has stated publicly that it does not work with Nexus, which is intended for its Xbox-licensed controllers.
FixUse GameSir Connect instead. Nexus not listing the controller is expected behaviour, not a fault. Connect also requires the controller to be in XInput mode via cable or dongle to list it.

Source: fixoryhq.com

Gyro, rear paddles or extra bumpers do nothing in a game
Likely causePlain XInput does not expose those inputs.
FixChoose the connection mode that exposes what you need rather than looking for a single setting, or clone the inputs onto standard buttons in GameSir Connect.

Source: fixoryhq.com

Intermittent disconnects or odd behaviour after raising the polling rate
Likely causeHigh polling rates can conflict with some USB paths and software.
FixSet the polling rate back to 250 Hz to confirm, then raise it again. Prefer rear motherboard USB ports and avoid hubs.

Source: gamesir.com

Sticks or triggers reading incorrectly
Likely causeCalibration data is off.
FixCalibrate from the app via the Test icon with Mode + Menu, or manually: hold View + Home + Menu until the Home indicator flashes slowly white, press A with nothing touched, then pull both triggers fully three times and rotate both sticks fully three times and press A.

Source: gamesir.com

Frequently asked questions

Which app manages the G7 Pro 8K?

GameSir Connect. This is the single most common source of confusion with this model — Nexus is for GameSir's Xbox-licensed controllers and will not list the 8K. Per GameSir's own downloads page, Connect covers the G7 Pro 8K PC editions (Aimlabs, Champion, Nioh 3, Royal2), Tarantula 8K PC, Tegenaria Lite, Super Nova, Nova Lite 2 and Cyclone 2.

Source: gamesir.com

Does it really reach 8000 Hz on wireless?

GameSir states 8000 Hz is supported on both wired and 2.4 GHz. Independent measurement of the Aimlabs Edition recorded a median around 7936 Hz on both cable and dongle.

Source: gamepadla.com

How do I toggle the stick deadzone?

Hold the M button and press in the left or right stick for 2 seconds to toggle that stick's deadzone on or off.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I enable zero-deadzone (Raw) mode?

Hold the Mode button and press in the left or right stick until the controller vibrates. This toggles between Raw mode (zero deadzone) and Circular stick trajectory.

Source: gamesir.com

Does it support 3.5 mm audio wirelessly?

Yes — GameSir states 3.5 mm audio output works over both wired and 2.4 GHz connections.

Source: gamesir.com
