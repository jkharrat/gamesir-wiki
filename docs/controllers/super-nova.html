GameSir Super Nova

A $49.99 tri-mode multiplatform controller with Hall Effect sticks and triggers, magnetic swappable faceplates, and an auto start-stop charging dock.

Mid-rangeGameSir Hall Effect (non-contact magnetic), with anti-friction glide rings — NOT TMR1000 HzGyroGameSir Connect (PC) and the GameSir app (mobile). Measured resolution8.0-bit, ~256 steps (gamepadla, dongle in DS4 mode, FW 1.0.6) — likely reflects the reporting mode rather than the sensor ceiling

Four gamepadla Stick Tracer runs on firmware 1.0.6 produced center errors from 0.0% to 2.3% on the left stick and 0.0% to 2.7% on the right. The 0.0% results were attributed to the controller's built-in inner deadzone masking the true figure. Unlike the G7 Pro family, the Super Nova does apply an inner deadzone, which is why apparent drift is less common on it.

Triggers, D-pad and buttons

Trigger technologyHall Effect analog triggers with a 100% response area
Trigger stopsYes
D-padMembrane D-pad. TechRadar's review specifically calls the D-pad the weakest part of the controller ('isn't best in class').
Face buttonsMembrane ABXY, marketed as noise-free/silent. The ABXY buttons are physically detachable so the layout can be rearranged between Xbox and Nintendo positions by hand. Button VALUES can be swapped separately in firmware by holding M + the south face button, which swaps A-B and X-Y.
Rear buttons2
Extra bumpers0

A physical trigger gear switch selects long-pull or short-pull position. After switching, GameSir instructs you to press the trigger fully three times so it auto-calibrates the new pull distance. Hair trigger mode is a separate software toggle: hold M + LT/RT, and it requires the trigger to be set to the long-pull position first.

Two rear buttons (L4/R4) supporting single-button mapping and macro recording, plus an M (mode/macro) button. The rear buttons ship as 'null inputs' with no function until mapped. On-controller mapping: hold M + L4/R4 until the corresponding RGB strip slowly flashes white, press the button to assign, then press L4/R4 again to save. Exiting without pressing a target button clears the assignment.

Connectivity and performance

Connection modesWired / 2.4 GHz / Bluetooth
Polling rate (PC)1000 Hz (wired and 2.4GHz dongle)
Polling rate (Xbox)Not documented
Audio jackNone
GyroYes
RumbleTwo rumble motors, one in each grip, described by GameSir as dual asymmetric motors. Vibration intensity is adjustable on the fly by holding M and pressing - or +.

Tri-mode: USB-C wired, 2.4GHz dongle, Bluetooth 5.0. A three-position rear mode switch selects 2.4G / Off / Bluetooth. Does NOT support Xbox consoles. GameSir's own manual lists platforms as 'PC, Switch, and Android' while the product page and retail listings add iOS; Switch 2 support is claimed on retail listings. Bluetooth connects to PC only as a DS4 device, which is why GameSir Connect cannot see it over Bluetooth.

Xbox consoles are not supported. Bluetooth is 125 Hz. Gamepadla measured 996-1000 Hz median wired, 999-1000 Hz via dongle, and 125-391 Hz over Bluetooth; measured button latency was 7.32 ms wired (XInput), 12.1-20.4 ms via dongle, and 14.5-26.9 ms over Bluetooth (GPDL method, firmware 1.0.6/1.3). Note that connecting by cable while in Switch mode drops polling to 125 Hz.

Gyro: Built-in 6-axis gyroscope. Calibrated on-device by placing the controller on a flat surface and holding M + Screenshot for 2 seconds; the Home light flashes red and blue alternately during the process.

Battery and build

Battery capacity1000mAh
Claimed battery lifeManufacturer estimate of about 15 hours. GameSir publishes no battery-life figure on its product page or manual; TechRadar states that GameSir supplied the 15-hour figure on request.
Swappable faceplatesYes

Third-party review estimates vary widely and are less reliable: roughly 16 hours at 50% RGB brightness and up to ~20 hours with RGB off (Techxreviews); some reseller spec tables claim 'up to 25 hours,' which is not supported by GameSir's own 15-hour estimate. There is no battery percentage readout in the software or on the controller — the only indicator is the Home light flashing red below 15%. Ships with a smart auto start-stop charging dock; lifting the controller can be set to power it on.

Magnetic detachable middle faceplate, sold in additional colors by GameSir. The ABXY buttons themselves are also detachable for layout rearrangement. Thin RGB light strips on the front sync with a matching strip on the charging dock.

Notable features

  • Hall Effect sticks AND Hall Effect triggers at a $49.99 price point
  • Magnetic detachable middle faceplate plus physically detachable, rearrangeable ABXY buttons
  • Smart auto start-stop charging dock included, with RGB that syncs to the controller
  • 1000 Hz polling over both wired and 2.4GHz connections
  • Trigger gear for long/short pull plus a separate software hair-trigger mode
  • Extensive on-controller configuration via M-button combos (turbo, deadzone, vibration intensity, RGB, layout swap) with no software required
  • Membrane ABXY marketed as noise-free/silent
  • Rubber-coated textured grips

Known issues and fixes

Documented problems with documented solutions. Each entry cites where the fix comes from.

GameSir Connect cannot see the Super Nova when it is connected over Bluetooth.
Likely causeBy design. Over Bluetooth the Super Nova presents to a PC only as a DS4 device, which GameSir Connect cannot access.
FixConnect by cable or 2.4GHz dongle in XInput mode instead. Bluetooth cannot be used with GameSir Connect at all.

Source: gamesir.com

The Super Nova appears in GameSir Connect's device list but still will not connect, even on wired or dongle mode.
Likely causeThe controller is in the wrong input mode, has the wrong USB PID selected, or a stale Windows driver is bound to it.
FixGameSir's documented sequence: (1) press [-] + [+] for 3 seconds to cycle to XInput mode — indicator lights 2 and 4 illuminate; (2) press M + Home for 3 seconds to switch PID; (3) press Logo + [+] for 5 seconds to force XInput mode; (4) uninstall the driver in Device Manager and scan for hardware changes.

Source: gamesir.com

The controller soft-locks and will not power on or off after pressing 'update firmware' in GameSir Connect.
Likely causeA known bug in the firmware update flow, reported for both the Super Nova and Cyclone 2.
FixPress the recessed Reset button inside the circular hole on the back of the controller using a paperclip-sized object to force it off. A separate settings/profile reset is done by holding Home + Menu until the Logo button blinks. Firmware can also be updated with GameSir's standalone manual updater: connect via USB, hold Home + X + Y for 3 seconds to enter update mode, then click update firmware.

Source: gamesir.com

Buttons fire repeatedly from a single press and the indicator light flashes red.
Likely causeTurbo (rapid-fire) mode has been enabled accidentally, usually by an M-button combo.
FixDouble-press the M button to clear all turbo settings. Turbo is toggled per-button by holding M and pressing A/B/X/Y/LB/RB/LT/RT.

Source: gamesir.com

The trigger lock is enabled but the trigger does not reach 100% output.
Likely causeGameSir states the trigger lock function is firmware-controlled and may not register the physical switch position on the first attempt.
FixToggle the trigger gear switch several times until the controller registers the setting. GameSir separately instructs pressing the trigger fully down three times after switching so the pull distance auto-calibrates.

Source: gamesir.com

The rear buttons do nothing at all when pressed.
Likely causeNot a fault. The rear extra buttons ship as 'null inputs' with no assigned function until they are remapped.
FixMap them via GameSir Connect, the GameSir mobile app, or on-controller: hold M + L4/R4 until the RGB strip flashes white, press the target button, then press L4/R4 again to save. To clear a mapping, press the same rear button again instead of a target. Note GameSir's manual page incorrectly names 'GameSir Nexus' here — Nexus does not support the Super Nova.

Source: gamesir.com

GameSir Connect is installed but does not detect the controller, and the app never updates itself.
Likely causeA Microsoft Store glitch prevents GameSir Connect from receiving automatic updates, leaving users on a version that predates Super Nova support.
FixUninstall and reinstall GameSir Connect from the Microsoft Store, or download it directly from GameSir's software page instead of relying on Store updates.

Source: gamesir.com

Frequently asked questions

Which app should I use — GameSir Connect, the GameSir app, or GameSir Nexus?

GameSir Connect on PC, or the GameSir app on mobile. GameSir Nexus does not support the Super Nova (it is for GameSir's Xbox-licensed controllers). Connect requires a wired or dongle connection in XInput mode; it cannot reach the controller over Bluetooth.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I pair the Super Nova to a Nintendo Switch?

On the Switch home screen open Controllers, then Change Grip/Order, and leave that page open. Set the rear mode switch to Bluetooth and press Home to power on. Hold Home + Y for 2 seconds until the channel indicator flashes up and down rapidly, then wait for the connection. If it fails, hold Home + Screenshot to force pairing mode and retry.

Source: gamesir.com

What are the Bluetooth mode combos for each platform?

With the mode switch in the Bluetooth position: Home + B for 2 seconds selects PC/other-device mode (appears as 'Wireless Controller'), Home + Y selects Switch mode, and Home + A selects Android mode (appears as 'GameSir-Nova Pro'). Home + Screenshot held 2-3 seconds forces pairing mode from any of these.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I calibrate the sticks and triggers?

Set the triggers to long-travel mode first. Hold [-] + [+] + Home for 2 seconds to enter calibration — the Home light flashes slowly in white. With nothing touching the sticks or triggers, press A once. Then rotate both sticks in full circles 2-3 times and fully press both triggers three times, and press A to exit.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I switch between XInput, Switch, and Android/other input modes?

Hold the [-] (View) and [+] (Menu) buttons to cycle through XInput, Switch and other-device modes. This only works in dongle and wired modes, not over Bluetooth. In XInput mode, indicator lights 2 and 4 illuminate.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I reset the controller?

There are two resets. For a hardware force-off when the controller will not power on or off, press the recessed Reset button in the circular hole on the back with a paperclip. To reset the profile and all settings, hold Home + Menu until the Logo button blinks (GameSir's support FAQ specifies 5 seconds, until the Logo LED flashes green twice).

Source: gamesir.com

How long does the battery last, and how do I check the level?

GameSir's estimate, provided to TechRadar on request, is about 15 hours from the 1000mAh cell; no figure is published on the product page. There is no percentage readout in the software or on the controller — the only battery indicator is the Home light flashing red when charge drops below 15%. The controller auto-powers-off after 10 minutes of inactivity.

Source: www.techradar.com

Does it work with Xbox consoles?

No. The Super Nova supports PC, Switch, Android and iOS only. Xbox consoles require an Xbox-licensed GameSir model.

Source: www.windowscentral.com

Can I change the button layout?

Yes, in two ways. Physically, both the middle faceplate and the ABXY buttons are detachable so you can rearrange the caps. In firmware, hold M + the south face button to swap the A-B and X-Y button values.

Source: gamesir.com
