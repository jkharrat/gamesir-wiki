Which app, and the exact platform wording

GameSir Nexus (available on both Xbox and PC via the Microsoft Store; a 'Legacy GameSir Nexus' build also exists). The G7 SE does not use GameSir Connect, which is the app for the G7 Pro line.

How these measurements were arrived at

Center error measured by gamepadla on firmware 6.5.6, single unit. GameSir publishes no cycle-life rating for the G7 SE sticks; the 5-million-cycle figure applies to the G7 HE only. Gamepadla also measured 0.0% circle error on both sticks, 4.6%/6.9% asymmetry, no inner deadzone, 0.6 mm outer deadzone, and no cardinal snapping.

Trigger detail

No physical trigger stops or trigger locks. A software/hardware hair-trigger mode is toggled by holding M + LT or M + RT for 2 seconds until the controller vibrates. GameSir claims '100% response area'. Trigger travel distance is not documented.

Mapping the extra buttons

Two rear paddles labelled L4 and R4, plus a front 'M' (Mode) button below the d-pad and a dedicated mic mute button. Each back button has a physical quick-latch slider that mechanically locks it out to prevent accidental presses. Remap on the fly: hold M + L4 or M + R4 for 3 seconds until the indicator blinks slowly, then tap the button you want to assign. Repeat and tap L4/R4 itself to clear the assignment.

Which modes work on which platform

Wired only, via a 3 m detachable USB-C (USB-A to USB-C) cable. No battery and no wireless mode of any kind. GameSir's manual tech-spec block also lists Android, but the FAQ and product page list only Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One and Windows 10/11, so Android support is unconfirmed.

Polling rate caveats

GameSir states the Xbox console enforces a 250 Hz cap, so 1000 Hz is a PC-only option. Per GameSir's help center, 500 Hz requires Windows 10 or newer, while 1000 Hz requires Windows 11, firmware 6.30 or higher, GameSir Nexus 1.2.9 or higher, and a reboot after first enabling it. Both 500 Hz and 1000 Hz disable the 3.5 mm headphone jack. Gamepadla measured a genuine 1000 Hz median (1000.29 Hz average) on firmware 6.6.4, though some community reports claim units cap near 500 Hz.

Gyro detail

GameSir's official gyroscope list names G7 Pro, G7 Pro Wuchang, G7 Pro Zenless Zone Zero, Tarantula Pro Xbox and T7 Pro Sugar Whirl among Xbox controllers. The G7 SE is not on that list, and gamepadla's verified specs also record no gyroscope.

Battery detail

Wired only. GameSir's spec sheet lists 'Battery: No' and gamepadla's verified spec list records 'No battery'. There is nothing to charge and no battery-life claim to attribute.

Faceplate detail

Magnetic swappable faceplate designed to accept paint. Faceplates are one of the few parts GameSir sells separately. G7 series faceplates fit the newer G7 Pro, but the G7 Pro's 3-part split faceplate does NOT fit the G7, G7 SE or G7 HE. Calibration must be performed with the faceplate installed or the stick range values will be wrong.

Known issues and fixes

Documented problems with documented fixes.

Controller is plugged in and the light is on, but no inputs register, or it repeatedly disconnects, or it will not power on at all.
Likely causePID recognition failure between the controller and host, or the controller being in the wrong input mode (XInput vs GIP).
FixWhile connected, hold the Xbox button + M button for 3 seconds (you should hear an indicator tone), then unplug and replug to switch back to the original PID and restore device recognition. If that fails, hold the Xbox button + Share button for 3 seconds while connected to force-switch between XInput and GIP modes. Also try motherboard-rear USB ports rather than case ports or hubs, and a known-good data cable.

Source: gamesir.com

Connection instability: random disconnects, failure to power on, or no input, on specific firmware builds.
Likely causeGameSir names firmware 6.33, 6.38 and 6.5.6 as known problematic versions for the G7 SE and G7 HE that cause connection-related issues.
FixCheck your version in GameSir Nexus (the upward arrow labelled 'Firmware Upgrade' at top right) or in Legacy GameSir Nexus (the 'Updates' button on the controller page), then update to a current stable build. If Nexus does not offer the update, GameSir provides a standalone manual upgrader for G7 SE/HE on PC.

Source: gamesir.com

The 3.5 mm headset jack goes completely dead: no game audio and no mic.
Likely causeSelecting a 500 Hz or 1000 Hz report rate disables the controller's onboard audio. This is by design, not a fault.
FixSet the report rate back to 250 Hz in GameSir Nexus on PC. GameSir support explicitly says the polling rate 'should be locked at 250hz to avoid potential problems' for headset use. Also ensure the headset is pushed fully flush into the jack, and on Xbox set Settings > General > Volume & audio output > Headset audio to 'Stereo uncompressed'.

Source: doc.xiaoji.com

The sticks appear to drift immediately out of the box after setting the initial deadzone to 0 in GameSir Nexus.
Likely causeNot hardware drift. A 0 deadzone is a true physical zero, which makes the stick extremely sensitive to the smallest residual movement.
FixGameSir's official answer: set the initial deadzone to roughly 3-5 instead of 0 and behaviour returns to normal.

Source: gamesir.com

Face buttons or the d-pad double-click or register the wrong input; a bumper auto-clicks by itself.
Likely causeCorrupted profile/mapping state or outdated firmware.
FixRun the function reset by holding the Xbox button + Menu button for 5 seconds while the controller is connected, then update the firmware to the latest version in GameSir Nexus (GameSir's page cites V6.40 as the target at the time of writing), using the standalone PC upgrader if Nexus does not offer it.

Source: gamesir.com

The controller becomes unresponsive or refuses to power on after a button combination was held while plugging in the USB cable.
Likely causeHolding Xbox + Menu, or View + Xbox + Menu, WHILE plugging in the cable puts the controller into DFU (firmware update) mode or manual calibration mode rather than normal operation. GameSir also notes that controllers with cable guards can feel fully seated when only half inserted.
FixUnplug, plug the cable in firmly with no buttons held until it audibly clicks, then hold the Xbox button to power on. If it stays unresponsive, perform a manual firmware upgrade from a Windows PC using GameSir's standalone G7 series upgrader package.

Source: gamesir.com

Controller works perfectly on PC but is not detected by the Xbox console at all.
Likely causeStale console state / cached USB enumeration.
FixFull power cycle: plug the controller into the console's rear USB port, hold the console power button for 10+ seconds until it fully shuts down, unplug the power cord for about 30 seconds, press the console power button 10 times to discharge residual power, then power back on with the controller still in the rear port.

Source: gamesir.com

Frequently asked questions

What is the correct stick and trigger calibration procedure for the G7 SE? (Do NOT use the G7 Pro procedure.)

Hold View + Menu + Xbox and, still holding, plug the USB-C cable into your PC; release once it is in. The indicator blinks slowly white. Without touching the triggers or sticks, press A and the indicator goes out. Now press LT and RT to full travel and rotate both sticks at maximum angle three times, then press A again — a solid white indicator confirms it. Unplug and replug before use. Calibrate with the faceplate installed or the range values come out wrong, and move slowly and steadily to avoid data-collection errors. Manual calibration often succeeds where the in-app one fails. This plug-in procedure belongs to the older G7 series (G7, G7 SE, G7 HE) only: GameSir warns that using it on a G7 Pro drops that controller into firmware update mode, after which it will not power on normally. The G7 Pro uses View + Menu + Home held for 2 seconds while already connected.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I reset the G7 SE to default settings?

Two documented options. Function reset: hold the Xbox button + Menu button for 5 seconds while connected, which also fixes bad key binds you cannot undo. Full profile/settings reset: hold View + Menu + M until the logo button blinks, then hold the Xbox button for 3 seconds; this resets the profile and all controller settings. You can also just reapply the default profile with M + Y.

Source: gamesir.com

Is the G7 SE wireless, and does it have a battery?

No. It is wired only, using a 3 m detachable USB-C cable, and it has no battery at all. GameSir's own spec sheet lists 'Battery: No', so there is no charge time or battery-life figure for this controller.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I get 1000 Hz polling, and why does my headset stop working when I do?

1000 Hz is PC-only; GameSir states the Xbox console enforces a 250 Hz cap. On PC you need Windows 11, G7 SE firmware 6.30 or higher, and GameSir Nexus 1.2.9 or higher; select the report rate under Profiles and restart the computer the first time. 500 Hz works on Windows 10 or higher and needs no script. Running either 500 Hz or 1000 Hz disables the controller's headphone jack, which is expected behaviour. GameSir recommends 500 Hz for general use and 1000 Hz for competitive play.

Source: doc.xiaoji.com

How do I remap the back buttons and switch profiles without software?

Remap: hold M + R4 (or M + L4) for 3 seconds until the indicator blinks slowly, then tap the button you want mapped to it; the indicator turns solid to confirm. Clear it: repeat the combo and tap R4/L4 itself. Profiles: M + Y is the default profile, M + B is Profile 1, M + A is Profile 2, M + X is Profile 3. The two quick-latch sliders on the back mechanically enable or disable the back buttons entirely.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I enable hair-trigger mode and control headset volume from the pad?

Hair trigger: hold M + LT or M + RT for 2 seconds until the controller vibrates; repeat to disable. Master volume: hold M + d-pad Up/Down (10 levels, with a beep at max and min). Game/chat balance: hold M + d-pad Left/Right (beeps at max, middle and min). A headset must be plugged into the 3.5 mm jack for volume control to do anything. The mic mute button turns the indicator red when muted; holding it temporarily unmutes.

Source: gamesir.com

What software does the G7 SE use, and what can it change?

GameSir Nexus, available on both Xbox and PC. It supports three custom profiles covering button mapping, d-pad diagonal tuning, stick and trigger fine-tuning (including stick curves, initial/max deadzone, anti-deadzone, hair-trigger static vs adaptive modes, and continuous trigger toggle), vibration configuration, report rate selection, and firmware updates. Sticks can be set to zero deadzone and switched between Default and GameSir's exclusive 'Raw' mode. Note the G7 SE uses Nexus, not GameSir Connect, which is the app for the G7 Pro family.

Source: gamesir.com

Does the G7 SE have a gyroscope?

No. GameSir's official gyro-equipped list covers the G7 Pro, G7 Pro Wuchang, G7 Pro Zenless Zone Zero, Tarantula Pro Xbox and T7 Pro Sugar Whirl on the Xbox side. The G7 SE is not included, and gamepadla's verified spec sheet also records no gyroscope.

Source: gamesir.com

Are G7 Pro faceplates compatible with the G7 SE?

No, not in that direction. The G7 Pro can accept faceplates from the older G7 series, but the G7 Pro's three-part split faceplate is not compatible with the G7, G7 SE or G7 HE. Magnetic faceplates for the G7 series are one of the few parts GameSir sells separately; internal components, buttons and USB ports are not sold as spares.

Source: gamesir.com
