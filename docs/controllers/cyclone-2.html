GameSir Cyclone 2

An Xbox-layout tri-mode wireless controller with Mag-Res TMR sticks, micro-switch face buttons and dual-mode Hall Effect triggers, measured among the lowest-latency controllers in its price class.

How these measurements were arrived at

A separate reviewer using gamepadla tooling measured 0.8-1.0%, so results vary between units and firmware. Right-stick asymmetry measured notably higher than the left, 10.2% against 3.4%.

Trigger detail

Physical trigger locks toggle between Hall Effect analog travel and mechanical micro-switch actuation. A separate software hair-trigger mode is available via key combo for FPS and action games. On-board calibration is supported for triggers, sticks and gyro.

Mapping the extra buttons

Two fully remappable back buttons plus an M button. Four hot-swappable profiles are stored on the controller itself, so settings persist without the software running; the active profile is shown by four illuminated segments in the center of the controller. Macro and turbo are supported.

RumbleTwo asymmetric rumble motors, one in each grip. Vibration intensity is adjustable via a button combo as well as in software. Reviewers also note wireless audio support alongside the 3.5mm jack.
Which modes work on which platform

Tri-mode: USB-C wired, 2.4GHz dongle, Bluetooth. Explicitly NOT compatible with Xbox consoles. Input modes are XInput, DS4, NS and HID, cycled by holding View + Menu. Switch 2 support is claimed on retail listings. The included aluminum charging dock has a USB port, and GameSir recommends plugging the dongle into the dock to keep it close to the controller.

Polling rate caveats

Xbox consoles are not supported. Bluetooth is rated 250 Hz and Gamepadla measured roughly 250-400 Hz there. This is the strongest-measuring controller covered on this site: Gamepadla recorded 2.33 ms average button latency and 4.70 ms stick latency wired with only 0.28 ms jitter, and 7.4 ms button latency via dongle, earning a LatScore of A wired / B wireless. Bluetooth averaged around 6 ms but with spikes over 26 ms and 3.85 ms jitter.

Gyro detail

6-axis gyroscope, active in Switch mode and manually activatable elsewhere. On-board gyro calibration is supported.

Battery detail

Independent measurements land at the low end of that estimate: roughly 8 hours with RGB and rumble active (Pocket-lint), and Neowin recorded approximately 8 hours minimum with lighting enabled. GameSir Connect shows only a color-coded battery indicator, not a percentage. Charges via USB-C or the included aluminum charging tower, which starts charging automatically when the controller is set down.

Faceplate detail

No swappable faceplate system. GameSir does sell detachable joystick caps for the Cyclone 2 as a separate accessory, and the stock caps are thick Sony-style with anti-friction metal necks. The shell uses translucent plastic with an RGB strip in each grip and around the center button.

Known issues and fixes

Documented problems with documented solutions. Each entry cites where the fix comes from.

The 2.4GHz dongle connection keeps dropping, sometimes with jittery button and stick input.
Likely causeMismatched controller and dongle firmware is the primary cause. GameSir also cites RF interference from 8K wireless devices and NearLink devices, and thick obstructions between the dongle and controller.
FixMatch the firmware: dongle V1.1.9 or above AND controller V3.4.4 or above — GameSir stresses that updating one requires updating the other. Update the dongle from GameSir Connect while the controller is connected through it, or force it by holding the dongle's sync button as you plug it in (the light should stay off) with Connect open. Update the controller by plugging it in via cable and pressing 'update firmware', or force it by holding Logo + Menu while plugging it in with Connect open. For interference, move the dongle closer using a USB extension or the charging dock's USB port.

Source: gamesir.com

The controller shows a blue rather than green indicator light when connected to a PC, and games behave incorrectly.
Likely causeThe controller has been accidentally switched into DS4 or NS input mode instead of XInput.
FixHold the View button + Menu button to cycle between XInput, NS and DS4 modes. XInput, indicated by a green light, is the correct mode for most PC use and is required for GameSir Connect.

Source: gamesir.com

The controller will not power on at all after a firmware upgrade failed in GameSir Connect.
Likely causeInterrupted or failed firmware flash leaving the controller soft-locked. TheSixthAxis describes this as a known bug affecting both the Cyclone 2 and Super Nova.
FixReset the controller by poking the reset hole on the back with a small object, then retry the update.

Source: gamesir.com

A single button press registers as continuous rapid presses and the light flashes red.
Likely causeTurbo (rapid-fire) mode is enabled.
FixDouble-tap the M button to turn turbo off.

Source: gamesir.com

Cannot connect over Bluetooth or dongle; the indicator light keeps blinking slowly.
Likely causeSlow blinking means reconnecting mode — the controller is trying to reach a previously paired device rather than accepting a new one.
FixHold the Home Button + Share Button for 2 seconds to force pairing mode; the light should then blink fast. For a dongle connection, also hold the pairing button on the dongle so the two re-pair.

Source: gamesir.com

GameSir Nexus does not recognize the Cyclone 2.
Likely causeWrong application — the Cyclone 2 is a GameSir Connect device. Nexus is only for GameSir's Xbox-licensed controllers.
FixUse GameSir Connect with the controller connected via dongle or cable in XInput mode.

Source: gamesir.com

Stick drift or trigger problems during use.
Likely causeSticks or triggers out of calibration.
FixRun the on-board Stick & Trigger Calibration procedure from the manual; GameSir links a Cyclone 2 specific calibration tutorial video from the FAQ page. Deadzones can also be tuned in GameSir Connect or on-board.

Source: gamesir.com

Frequently asked questions

Which software does the Cyclone 2 use?

GameSir Connect, connected via dongle or cable in XInput mode. GameSir Nexus does not support it. The Cyclone 2 is explicitly listed among GameSir Connect's compatible products on GameSir's downloads page.

Source: gamesir.com

What platforms does the Cyclone 2 support?

Switch, iOS, Android, PC and Steam. It is NOT compatible with Xbox consoles.

Source: gamesir.com

What firmware versions do I need for reliable dongle operation?

Dongle V1.1.9 or above and controller V3.4.4 or above. GameSir stresses these must be updated together — updating the dongle without updating the controller to the latest version causes disconnections.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I switch between XInput, Nintendo Switch and DS4 input modes?

Hold the View button + Menu button. XInput, shown by a green indicator light, is the standard mode for PC.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I force the controller back into pairing mode?

Hold the Home Button + Share Button for 2 seconds; the indicator should switch from slow blinking to fast blinking. For dongle pairing, also press the pairing button on the dongle.

Source: gamesir.com

How are the triggers configured?

The Hall Effect analog triggers give linear input suited to racing games. A key combo switches them to hair-trigger mode for FPS and action games, and physical trigger locks toggle between Hall Effect analog and micro-switch actuation.

Source: gamesir.com

How many mappable buttons does the Cyclone 2 have?

Two mappable back buttons. The controller also stores four hot-swappable profiles on-board, indicated by the four lit segments in the center.

Source: gamesir.com

How long does the battery last?

GameSir estimates about 10 hours from the 860mAh cell, provided to TechRadar on request. Independent testing lands nearer 8 hours with RGB and rumble active. GameSir Connect shows only a color-coded indicator rather than a percentage.

Source: www.techradar.com

Does it have a headphone jack?

Yes, a 3.5mm headphone jack on the bottom edge. TechRadar notes this as a bonus over the Super Nova, which lacks one.

Source: www.neowin.net
