Connection support is platform-dependent: wired only for Xbox consoles, wired or 2.4 GHz for PC, Bluetooth 5.3 for Android.

Xbox consoles enforce a 250 Hz cap, so 1000 Hz is a PC-only capability. Reaching 1000 Hz on PC requires the controller to switch from GIP to XInput mode.

Gyro: PC only. Can be mapped to stick, buttons or mouse via GameSir Nexus.

Known issues and fixes

Documented problems with documented solutions. Each entry cites where the fix comes from.

Sticks appear to drift even though the controller is nearly new
Likely causeTMR sticks are magnetic and do not wear like potentiometers, so this is rarely mechanical. The G7 Pro applies no inner deadzone, so its natural center error is visible to games. A misconfigured anti-deadzone in Nexus can also produce it — GameSir's own documentation states improper anti-deadzone configuration 'may appear similar to stick drift'.
FixSet Anti-deadzone Initial back to 0 in Nexus, confirm you are not in Raw (zero deadzone) trajectory mode, recalibrate, then add a small Initial deadzone only if needed.

Source: gamesir.com

Stick ranges are wrong or inconsistent after calibration
Likely causeCalibration performed with the magnetic faceplate removed.
FixGameSir documentation states calibration must be performed with the faceplate installed; calibrating without it produces incorrect range values. Reinstall the faceplate and recalibrate.

Source: gamesir.com

Sticks feel too fast or too twitchy after calibrating
Likely causeRotation speed during calibration affects the resulting sensitivity.
FixRecalibrate using slow, steady, gentle full rotations. GameSir notes faster rotation during calibration yields higher sensitivity than slower rotation.

Source: gamesir.com

Controller shows in Windows as 'Xbox 360 Controller' and disconnects when GameSir Nexus is closed
Likely causeExpected behaviour. 1000 Hz polling and keyboard/mouse bindings require the controller to switch protocol from GIP (Xbox Gaming Device) to XInput, which renames the device and forces a brief reconnect.
FixThis is not a fault. The mode can be switched manually by holding Xbox + Share for about 3 seconds.

Source: gamesir.com

Controller is powered on and connected but invisible to games and to GameSir Nexus
Likely causeThe controller is stuck in HID (Android support) mode.
FixHold Xbox + Mode for roughly 4 seconds until the Xbox indicator blinks twice to reset the PID. Alternatively hold Share + Menu for about 4 seconds to toggle the mode.

Source: gamesir.com

Controller will not power on after attempting calibration found in an older guide or video
Likely causeThe calibration procedure for the older G7 / G7 SE / G7 HE puts a G7 Pro into firmware update mode instead of calibration mode.
FixDo not use the older G7 sequence. Use View + Xbox + Menu on the G7 Pro. If the controller is already stuck, GameSir directs users to perform a manual firmware upgrade from a Windows PC.

Source: gamesir.com

Set polling to 1000 Hz but measured rate is lower
Likely causeThe controller has not completed the switch into XInput mode, or the USB path cannot sustain the rate.
FixFully close Nexus to trigger the mode switch, or hold Xbox + Share for 3 seconds to force it. Prefer rear motherboard USB ports over front-panel ports and hubs, and use a known data-capable cable. Two indicator blinks mean the mode swap failed.

Source: gamesir.com

Frequently asked questions

How do I calibrate the sticks and triggers?

With the faceplate installed and the controller connected, hold View + Xbox + Menu until the Xbox indicator flashes slowly white. Without touching the sticks or triggers, press A — the indicator turns off. Then pull LT and RT to full travel three times and rotate both sticks at full extent three times, and press A. A solid white indicator confirms completion. Rotate slowly and steadily. Calibration affects analog trigger mode only, not mouse-click mode.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I reset all profiles and settings?

Hold View + Mode + Menu. The Xbox LED blinks three times to confirm all profiles and custom settings have been cleared. Individual profiles can also be reset from within GameSir Nexus.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I map the rear buttons and mini bumpers?

They are null inputs with no default function. Hold Mode + the remappable button until the indicator blinks, then press the target button to assign it. To clear a mapping, repeat and press the same remappable button again. They can also be configured in GameSir Nexus.

Source: gamesir.com

Which app does the G7 Pro use?

GameSir Nexus, available from the Xbox and Windows stores. Note that the G7 Pro 8K PC uses GameSir Connect instead — the two apps do not cover each other's hardware.

Source: gamesir.com

Can I get 1000 Hz on an Xbox console?

No. Xbox consoles enforce a 250 Hz polling limit, so the 1000 Hz option is available on PC only.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I switch preset profiles?

Mode + Y selects configuration 1, Mode + B selects 2, Mode + A selects 3, Mode + X selects 4.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I enable hair trigger mode?

Hold Mode + LT or RT for 2 seconds to toggle hair trigger mode on or off.

Source: gamesir.com

The gyro is behaving incorrectly. How do I recalibrate it?

Place the controller flat on a level surface and hold Mode + Share for 3 seconds. The logo LED flashes orange and white alternately during calibration.

Source: gamesir.com
