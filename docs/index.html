Start here

Three things account for most GameSir support questions.

“My controller isn't detected by the app” — check which app it needs

GameSir ships two PC apps and they cover different hardware. Nexus handles the Xbox-licensed controllers: per GameSir's downloads page, the G7 Pro and its licensed editions, G7 SE, G7 HE, T7, T7 Pro, Kaleid, Kaleid Flux and Tarantula Pro for Xbox. Connect handles everything else, including the G7 Pro 8K PC editions, Tarantula 8K PC, Tegenaria Lite, Super Nova, Nova Lite 2 and Cyclone 2.

The trap is that one name can appear in both families. Tarantula Pro for Xbox uses Nexus; the multiplatform Tarantula Pro and the Tarantula 8K PC use Connect. Waiting in Nexus for a Connect device to show up is one of the most common reasons a controller is never detected, and it is expected behaviour rather than a fault.

On mobile, the separate GameSir app covers the Super Nova, Nova Lite, Nova Lite 2, G8 series and X-series, among others. Your model's page says which app applies.

“My drift-proof controller is drifting” — usually configuration, not hardware

Hall Effect and TMR sticks sense magnetically, with nothing touching, so they do not wear into drift the way potentiometer sticks do. When one seems to drift, it is usually that no inner deadzone is applied and the game is seeing the stick's natural centre error.

A misconfigured anti-deadzone looks the same. GameSir's own documentation says it “may appear similar to stick drift”. Set it back to zero before assuming the hardware has failed — see the drift section.

“It shows up as an Xbox 360 Controller and disconnects” — that's intended

High polling rates and keyboard/mouse bindings need the controller to switch from GIP (Xbox Gaming Device) to XInput. Windows renames the device and it drops off for a moment while it switches. That is normal.

Unofficial and community-maintained. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by GameSir, and nothing here is for sale. Specifications come from GameSir's product pages, manuals and FAQ documents, from independent measurements published by gamepadla.com, and from published reviews. Unless a figure says otherwise, none of it is first-hand testing. Manufacturer claims are labelled as claims. Anything unverified reads “not documented”. Prices are historical launch figures, not current offers.

Found an error? Open an issue or send a pull request with a source. Contributors are credited in the commit history.
