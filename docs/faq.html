Frequently asked questions

Questions collected from GameSir's official FAQ pages and manuals, grouped across every covered model and searchable in one place. Each answer links to its source.

What is the correct stick and trigger calibration procedure for the G7 HE? (Do NOT use the G7 Pro procedure.) GameSir G7 HE

1) Hold View + Menu + Xbox buttons and, while still holding, insert the USB-C cable into your PC's USB port; release after plugging in. The indicator blinks slowly in white. 2) Without touching LT, RT or either stick, press A. The indicator goes out. 3) Press LT and RT to maximum travel and rotate both sticks at maximum angle three times, then press A. The indicator turns solid white to confirm completion. 4) Unplug and replug before using. Caveats: calibration must be performed with the faceplate installed or range values will be incorrect; move at a steady, gentle speed; manual calibration often works when in-app calibration fails; calibration applies only to analog trigger mode. CRITICAL: this hold-while-plugging-in procedure belongs to the older G7 series (G7 / G7 SE / G7 HE). GameSir explicitly warns that applying this older-G7 calibration procedure to a G7 Pro will put that controller into firmware update mode and leave it unable to power on normally. The G7 Pro instead uses View + Menu + Home held 2 seconds while already connected.

Source: gamesir.com

What exactly is different between the G7 HE and the G7 SE? GameSir G7 HE

Both are wired-only, share the same 152 x 103 x 63 mm shell, the same GameSir Hall Effect sticks and Hall Effect analog triggers, the same four rumble motors, the same two back buttons, the same 3.5 mm jack and mute button, the same magnetic faceplate, and the same GameSir Nexus software. The differences: the HE uses microswitch ABXY and a microswitch d-pad (3 million press rating) where the SE uses membrane; the HE adds rubberized silicone grips; the HE removes the SE's two physical back-button lock latches; the HE weighs 227 g versus the SE's 221 g; and the HE is $49.99 versus the SE's $44.99. GameSir's own comparison FAQ answer is published only as an image with no text, so this comparison is assembled from the two product spec sheets plus PCGamingWiki and Windows Central.

Source: gamesir.com

Is the G7 HE wireless, and does it have a battery? GameSir G7 HE

No. It is wired only, using a 3 m detachable USB-C cable, and GameSir's spec sheet lists 'Battery: No'. There is nothing to charge and no battery-life figure of any kind. Windows Central's main criticism of the controller is precisely that it remains wired at a $49.99 price in 2024.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I reset the G7 HE to default settings? GameSir G7 HE

Function reset: hold the Xbox button + Menu button for 5 seconds while connected. This is also GameSir's documented fix for a key bind you set incorrectly in Nexus and cannot undo, and for buttons registering double or wrong inputs. Full reset: hold View + Menu + M until the logo button blinks, then hold the Xbox button for 3 seconds, which resets the profile and all controller settings. You can also simply reapply the default profile with M + Y.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I enable hair-trigger mode, switch profiles, and remap the back buttons? GameSir G7 HE

Hair trigger: hold M + LT or M + RT for 2 seconds until the corresponding trigger vibrates. Profiles: M + Y for default, M + B for Profile 1, M + A for Profile 2, M + X for Profile 3. Back buttons: hold M + R4 or M + L4 for 3 seconds until the indicator blinks slowly, then tap the button you want assigned; the indicator turns solid to confirm. To cancel, repeat the combo and tap R4/L4 itself.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I control headset volume and the game/chat mix from the controller? GameSir G7 HE

A headset must be plugged into the 3.5 mm jack. Hold M + d-pad Up/Down to raise or lower master volume; the G7 HE connection tutorial documents 11 levels while the manual says 10, with a beep at maximum and minimum. Hold M + d-pad Left/Right to shift the balance between game volume and voice chat volume, with a beep at maximum, middle and minimum. Press the mic mute button to mute (indicator turns solid red) and press again to unmute; holding it while muted temporarily unmutes.

Source: gamesir.com

Can the G7 HE reach 1000 Hz polling on an Xbox console? GameSir G7 HE

No. GameSir states the Xbox console enforces a 250 Hz polling rate limit, so the 1000 Hz option is only available on PC. GameSir additionally warns that any polling rate above 250 Hz disables the onboard 3.5 mm audio, and support recommends locking to 250 Hz if you use a headset through the controller.

Source: gamesir.com

Does the G7 HE have a gyroscope or trigger stops? GameSir G7 HE

GameSir's official gyro-equipped Xbox controller list covers the G7 Pro, G7 Pro Wuchang, G7 Pro Zenless Zone Zero, Tarantula Pro Xbox and T7 Pro Sugar Whirl, and the G7 HE is not among them; gamepadla's verified specs also record no gyroscope and no trigger lock. What can I configure in GameSir Nexus for the G7 HE? GameSir G7 HE

Three controller profiles covering button mapping, stick and trigger fine-tuning, vibration and more. For sticks: stick curves, initial and max deadzone, anti-deadzone, a true zero deadzone option, and a switch between Default and GameSir's exclusive 'Raw' mode. For triggers: impulse triggers for all games, hair trigger in Static mode (any movement in range reads as a full 100% pull) or Adaptive mode (100% on entering the range, instantly dropping to 0 on minimal release), and a Continuous Trigger toggle that holds an input until pressed again. Firmware updates also run through Nexus, with a standalone PC upgrader as a fallback.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I calibrate the sticks and triggers? GameSir G7 Pro

With the faceplate installed and the controller connected, hold View + Xbox + Menu until the Xbox indicator flashes slowly white. Without touching the sticks or triggers, press A — the indicator turns off. Then pull LT and RT to full travel three times and rotate both sticks at full extent three times, and press A. A solid white indicator confirms completion. Rotate slowly and steadily. Calibration affects analog trigger mode only, not mouse-click mode.

How do I reset all profiles and settings? GameSir G7 Pro

Hold View + Mode + Menu. The Xbox LED blinks three times to confirm all profiles and custom settings have been cleared. Individual profiles can also be reset from within GameSir Nexus.

How do I get 1000 Hz polling, and why does my headset stop working when I do? GameSir G7 SE

1000 Hz is PC-only; GameSir states the Xbox console enforces a 250 Hz cap. On PC you need Windows 11, G7 SE firmware 6.30 or higher, and GameSir Nexus 1.2.9 or higher; select the report rate under Profiles and restart the computer the first time. 500 Hz works on Windows 10 or higher and needs no script. Running either 500 Hz or 1000 Hz disables the controller's headphone jack, which is expected behaviour. GameSir recommends 500 Hz for general use and 1000 Hz for competitive play.

Source: doc.xiaoji.com

How do I remap the back buttons and switch profiles without software? GameSir G7 SE

Remap: hold M + R4 (or M + L4) for 3 seconds until the indicator blinks slowly, then tap the button you want mapped to it; the indicator turns solid to confirm. Clear it: repeat the combo and tap R4/L4 itself. Profiles: M + Y is the default profile, M + B is Profile 1, M + A is Profile 2, M + X is Profile 3. The two quick-latch sliders on the back mechanically enable or disable the back buttons entirely.

Are G7 Pro faceplates compatible with the G7 SE? GameSir G7 SE

No, not in that direction. The G7 Pro can accept faceplates from the older G7 series, but the G7 Pro's three-part split faceplate is not compatible with the G7, G7 SE or G7 HE. Magnetic faceplates for the G7 series are one of the few parts GameSir sells separately; internal components, buttons and USB ports are not sold as spares.
