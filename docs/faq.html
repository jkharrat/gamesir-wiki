FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Questions from GameSir's official FAQ pages and manuals, gathered across every covered model and searchable in one place.

Reading the button combinations

Most answers below are a button combination. Two figures cover nearly all of them: which control is which, and the remapping gesture shared across the range.

Every question

Which software does the Cyclone 2 use? GameSir Cyclone 2

GameSir Connect, connected via dongle or cable in XInput mode. GameSir Nexus does not support it. The Cyclone 2 is explicitly listed among GameSir Connect's compatible products on GameSir's downloads page.

Source: gamesir.com

What platforms does the Cyclone 2 support? GameSir Cyclone 2

Switch, iOS, Android, PC and Steam. It is NOT compatible with Xbox consoles.

Source: gamesir.com

What firmware versions do I need for reliable dongle operation? GameSir Cyclone 2

Dongle V1.1.9 or above and controller V3.4.4 or above. GameSir stresses these must be updated together — updating the dongle without updating the controller to the latest version causes disconnections.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I switch between XInput, Nintendo Switch and DS4 input modes? GameSir Cyclone 2

Hold the View button + Menu button. XInput, shown by a green indicator light, is the standard mode for PC.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I force the controller back into pairing mode? GameSir Cyclone 2

Hold the Home Button + Share Button for 2 seconds; the indicator should switch from slow blinking to fast blinking. For dongle pairing, also press the pairing button on the dongle.

Source: gamesir.com

How are the triggers configured? GameSir Cyclone 2

The Hall Effect analog triggers give linear input suited to racing games. A key combo switches them to hair-trigger mode for FPS and action games, and physical trigger locks toggle between Hall Effect analog and micro-switch actuation.

Source: gamesir.com

How many mappable buttons does the Cyclone 2 have? GameSir Cyclone 2

Two mappable back buttons. The controller also stores four hot-swappable profiles on-board, indicated by the four lit segments in the center.

Source: gamesir.com

How long does the battery last? GameSir Cyclone 2

GameSir estimates about 10 hours from the 860mAh cell, provided to TechRadar on request. Independent testing lands nearer 8 hours with RGB and rumble active. GameSir Connect shows only a color-coded indicator rather than a percentage.

Source: www.techradar.com

Does it have a headphone jack? GameSir Cyclone 2

Yes, a 3.5mm headphone jack on the bottom edge. TechRadar notes this as a bonus over the Super Nova, which lacks one.

Source: www.neowin.net

What is the correct stick and trigger calibration procedure for the G7 HE? (Do NOT use the G7 Pro procedure.) GameSir G7 HE

Hold View + Menu + Xbox and, still holding, plug the USB-C cable into your PC; release once it is in. The indicator blinks slowly white. Without touching LT, RT or either stick, press A and the indicator goes out. Now press LT and RT to full travel and rotate both sticks at maximum angle three times, then press A again — a solid white indicator confirms it. Unplug and replug before using. Calibrate with the faceplate installed or the range values come out wrong, move slowly and steadily, and note that this only affects analog trigger mode. Manual calibration often works where the in-app one fails. This plug-in procedure belongs to the older G7 series (G7, G7 SE, G7 HE) only: GameSir warns that using it on a G7 Pro drops that controller into firmware update mode, after which it will not power on normally. The G7 Pro uses View + Menu + Home held 2 seconds while already connected.

Source: gamesir.com

What exactly is different between the G7 HE and the G7 SE? GameSir G7 HE

Almost everything is shared: both are wired-only, in the same 152 x 103 x 63 mm shell, with the same Hall Effect sticks and analog triggers, four rumble motors, two back buttons, 3.5 mm jack and mute button, magnetic faceplate and Nexus software. Five things differ. The HE has microswitch ABXY and a microswitch d-pad rated for 3 million presses where the SE has membrane; it adds rubberized silicone grips; it drops the SE's two physical back-button lock latches; it weighs 227 g against 221 g; and it launched at $49.99 against $44.99. GameSir publishes its own comparison as an image with no text, so this one is assembled from the two spec sheets plus PCGamingWiki and Windows Central.

Source: gamesir.com

Is the G7 HE wireless, and does it have a battery? GameSir G7 HE

No. It is wired only, using a 3 m detachable USB-C cable, and GameSir's spec sheet lists 'Battery: No'. There is nothing to charge and no battery-life figure of any kind. Windows Central's main criticism of the controller is precisely that it remains wired at a $49.99 price in 2024.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I reset the G7 HE to default settings? GameSir G7 HE

Function reset: hold the Xbox button + Menu button for 5 seconds while connected. This is also GameSir's documented fix for a key bind you set incorrectly in Nexus and cannot undo, and for buttons registering double or wrong inputs. Full reset: hold View + Menu + M until the logo button blinks, then hold the Xbox button for 3 seconds, which resets the profile and all controller settings. You can also simply reapply the default profile with M + Y.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I enable hair-trigger mode, switch profiles, and remap the back buttons? GameSir G7 HE

Hair trigger: hold M + LT or M + RT for 2 seconds until the corresponding trigger vibrates. Profiles: M + Y for default, M + B for Profile 1, M + A for Profile 2, M + X for Profile 3. Back buttons: hold M + R4 or M + L4 for 3 seconds until the indicator blinks slowly, then tap the button you want assigned; the indicator turns solid to confirm. To cancel, repeat the combo and tap R4/L4 itself.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I control headset volume and the game/chat mix from the controller? GameSir G7 HE

A headset must be plugged into the 3.5 mm jack. Hold M + d-pad Up/Down to raise or lower master volume; the G7 HE connection tutorial documents 11 levels while the manual says 10, with a beep at maximum and minimum. Hold M + d-pad Left/Right to shift the balance between game volume and voice chat volume, with a beep at maximum, middle and minimum. Press the mic mute button to mute (indicator turns solid red) and press again to unmute; holding it while muted temporarily unmutes.

Source: gamesir.com

Can the G7 HE reach 1000 Hz polling on an Xbox console? GameSir G7 HE

No. GameSir states the Xbox console enforces a 250 Hz polling rate limit, so the 1000 Hz option is only available on PC. GameSir additionally warns that any polling rate above 250 Hz disables the onboard 3.5 mm audio, and support recommends locking to 250 Hz if you use a headset through the controller.

Source: gamesir.com

Does the G7 HE have a gyroscope or trigger stops? GameSir G7 HE

Neither. GameSir's official gyro-equipped Xbox controller list covers the G7 Pro, G7 Pro Wuchang, G7 Pro Zenless Zone Zero, Tarantula Pro Xbox and T7 Pro Sugar Whirl, and the G7 HE is not among them; gamepadla's verified specs also record no gyroscope and no trigger lock. Windows Central lists the absence of trigger stops as a notable omission for FPS players.

Source: gamesir.com

What can I configure in GameSir Nexus for the G7 HE? GameSir G7 HE

Three controller profiles covering button mapping, stick and trigger tuning and vibration. Sticks get curves, initial and max deadzone, anti-deadzone, a true zero deadzone option, and a switch between Default and GameSir's 'Raw' mode. Triggers get impulse triggers for all games, a hair trigger in Static mode (any movement in range reads as a full 100% pull) or Adaptive mode (100% on entering the range, dropping to 0 on the slightest release), and a Continuous Trigger toggle that holds an input until pressed again. Firmware updates also run through Nexus, with a standalone PC upgrader as a fallback.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I calibrate the sticks and triggers? GameSir G7 Pro

With the faceplate installed and the controller connected, hold View + Xbox + Menu until the Xbox indicator flashes slowly white. Without touching the sticks or triggers, press A — the indicator turns off. Then pull LT and RT to full travel three times and rotate both sticks at full extent three times, and press A. A solid white indicator confirms completion. Rotate slowly and steadily. Calibration affects analog trigger mode only, not mouse-click mode.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I reset all profiles and settings? GameSir G7 Pro

Hold View + Mode + Menu. The Xbox LED blinks three times to confirm all profiles and custom settings have been cleared. Individual profiles can also be reset from within GameSir Nexus.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I map the rear buttons and mini bumpers? GameSir G7 Pro

They are null inputs with no default function. Hold Mode + the remappable button until the indicator blinks, then press the target button to assign it. To clear a mapping, repeat and press the same remappable button again. They can also be configured in GameSir Nexus.

Source: gamesir.com

Which app does the G7 Pro use? GameSir G7 Pro

GameSir Nexus, available from the Xbox and Windows stores. Note that the G7 Pro 8K PC uses GameSir Connect instead — the two apps do not cover each other's hardware.

Source: gamesir.com

Can I get 1000 Hz on an Xbox console? GameSir G7 Pro

No. Xbox consoles enforce a 250 Hz polling limit, so the 1000 Hz option is available on PC only.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I switch preset profiles? GameSir G7 Pro

Mode + Y selects configuration 1, Mode + B selects 2, Mode + A selects 3, Mode + X selects 4.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I enable hair trigger mode? GameSir G7 Pro

Hold Mode + LT or RT for 2 seconds to toggle hair trigger mode on or off.

Source: gamesir.com

The gyro is behaving incorrectly. How do I recalibrate it? GameSir G7 Pro

Place the controller flat on a level surface and hold Mode + Share for 3 seconds. The logo LED flashes orange and white alternately during calibration.

Source: gamesir.com

Which app manages the G7 Pro 8K? GameSir G7 Pro 8K

GameSir Connect. This is the single most common source of confusion with this model — Nexus is for GameSir's Xbox-licensed controllers and will not list the 8K. Per GameSir's own downloads page, Connect covers the G7 Pro 8K PC editions (Aimlabs, Champion, Nioh 3, Royal2), Tarantula 8K PC, Tegenaria Lite, Super Nova, Nova Lite 2 and Cyclone 2.

Source: gamesir.com

Does it really reach 8000 Hz on wireless? GameSir G7 Pro 8K

GameSir states 8000 Hz is supported on both wired and 2.4 GHz. Independent measurement of the Aimlabs Edition recorded a median around 7936 Hz on both cable and dongle.

Source: gamepadla.com

How do I toggle the stick deadzone? GameSir G7 Pro 8K

Hold the M button and press in the left or right stick for 2 seconds to toggle that stick's deadzone on or off.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I enable zero-deadzone (Raw) mode? GameSir G7 Pro 8K

Hold the Mode button and press in the left or right stick until the controller vibrates. This toggles between Raw mode (zero deadzone) and Circular stick trajectory.

Source: gamesir.com

Does it support 3.5 mm audio wirelessly? GameSir G7 Pro 8K

Yes — GameSir states 3.5 mm audio output works over both wired and 2.4 GHz connections.

Source: gamesir.com

What is the correct stick and trigger calibration procedure for the G7 SE? (Do NOT use the G7 Pro procedure.) GameSir G7 SE

Hold View + Menu + Xbox and, still holding, plug the USB-C cable into your PC; release once it is in. The indicator blinks slowly white. Without touching the triggers or sticks, press A and the indicator goes out. Now press LT and RT to full travel and rotate both sticks at maximum angle three times, then press A again — a solid white indicator confirms it. Unplug and replug before use. Calibrate with the faceplate installed or the range values come out wrong, and move slowly and steadily to avoid data-collection errors. Manual calibration often succeeds where the in-app one fails. This plug-in procedure belongs to the older G7 series (G7, G7 SE, G7 HE) only: GameSir warns that using it on a G7 Pro drops that controller into firmware update mode, after which it will not power on normally. The G7 Pro uses View + Menu + Home held for 2 seconds while already connected.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I reset the G7 SE to default settings? GameSir G7 SE

Two documented options. Function reset: hold the Xbox button + Menu button for 5 seconds while connected, which also fixes bad key binds you cannot undo. Full profile/settings reset: hold View + Menu + M until the logo button blinks, then hold the Xbox button for 3 seconds; this resets the profile and all controller settings. You can also just reapply the default profile with M + Y.

Source: gamesir.com

Is the G7 SE wireless, and does it have a battery? GameSir G7 SE

No. It is wired only, using a 3 m detachable USB-C cable, and it has no battery at all. GameSir's own spec sheet lists 'Battery: No', so there is no charge time or battery-life figure for this controller.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I get 1000 Hz polling, and why does my headset stop working when I do? GameSir G7 SE

1000 Hz is PC-only; GameSir states the Xbox console enforces a 250 Hz cap. On PC you need Windows 11, G7 SE firmware 6.30 or higher, and GameSir Nexus 1.2.9 or higher; select the report rate under Profiles and restart the computer the first time. 500 Hz works on Windows 10 or higher and needs no script. Running either 500 Hz or 1000 Hz disables the controller's headphone jack, which is expected behaviour. GameSir recommends 500 Hz for general use and 1000 Hz for competitive play.

Source: doc.xiaoji.com

How do I remap the back buttons and switch profiles without software? GameSir G7 SE

Remap: hold M + R4 (or M + L4) for 3 seconds until the indicator blinks slowly, then tap the button you want mapped to it; the indicator turns solid to confirm. Clear it: repeat the combo and tap R4/L4 itself. Profiles: M + Y is the default profile, M + B is Profile 1, M + A is Profile 2, M + X is Profile 3. The two quick-latch sliders on the back mechanically enable or disable the back buttons entirely.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I enable hair-trigger mode and control headset volume from the pad? GameSir G7 SE

Hair trigger: hold M + LT or M + RT for 2 seconds until the controller vibrates; repeat to disable. Master volume: hold M + d-pad Up/Down (10 levels, with a beep at max and min). Game/chat balance: hold M + d-pad Left/Right (beeps at max, middle and min). A headset must be plugged into the 3.5 mm jack for volume control to do anything. The mic mute button turns the indicator red when muted; holding it temporarily unmutes.

Source: gamesir.com

What software does the G7 SE use, and what can it change? GameSir G7 SE

GameSir Nexus, available on both Xbox and PC. It supports three custom profiles covering button mapping, d-pad diagonal tuning, stick and trigger fine-tuning (including stick curves, initial/max deadzone, anti-deadzone, hair-trigger static vs adaptive modes, and continuous trigger toggle), vibration configuration, report rate selection, and firmware updates. Sticks can be set to zero deadzone and switched between Default and GameSir's exclusive 'Raw' mode. Note the G7 SE uses Nexus, not GameSir Connect, which is the app for the G7 Pro family.

Source: gamesir.com

Does the G7 SE have a gyroscope? GameSir G7 SE

No. GameSir's official gyro-equipped list covers the G7 Pro, G7 Pro Wuchang, G7 Pro Zenless Zone Zero, Tarantula Pro Xbox and T7 Pro Sugar Whirl on the Xbox side. The G7 SE is not included, and gamepadla's verified spec sheet also records no gyroscope.

Source: gamesir.com

Are G7 Pro faceplates compatible with the G7 SE? GameSir G7 SE

No, not in that direction. The G7 Pro can accept faceplates from the older G7 series, but the G7 Pro's three-part split faceplate is not compatible with the G7, G7 SE or G7 HE. Magnetic faceplates for the G7 series are one of the few parts GameSir sells separately; internal components, buttons and USB ports are not sold as spares.

Source: gamesir.com

Is the Kaleid wired or wireless? GameSir Kaleid

Wired only, with a 3 m detachable USB-C cable. There is no battery and no Bluetooth or 2.4 GHz option, and the same is true of the Kaleid Flux.

Source: gamesir.com

How does the Kaleid Flux differ from the Kaleid? GameSir Kaleid

GameSir calls them the same controller with nuanced variations. The Kaleid has microswitch ABXY; the Flux has membrane. Their printed PCB patterns and default RGB colours differ too: per GameSir's launch press release, the Kaleid ships rainbow with 'KALEID' printed on the PCB, the Flux ships 'noble gold' with a damascus pattern. They launched at $49.99 and $46.99. Sticks, triggers, motors, back buttons, audio jack, dimensions, weight and software are identical. Their firmware is not, and must never be cross-flashed.

Source: gamesir.com

Which app do I need for the Kaleid? GameSir Kaleid

GameSir Nexus, from the Microsoft Store on PC or Xbox. GameSir's download page lists Kaleid and Kaleid Flux under Nexus. GameSir Connect is for PC-only controllers such as the G7 Pro 8K and Cyclone 2, and the GameSir mobile app is for mobile controllers — installing the wrong one is a common reason the controller appears 'not detected'.

Source: gamesir.com

Is the Kaleid the same as the T4 Kaleid? GameSir Kaleid

No. GameSir explicitly warns that the T4 Kaleid (T4K), Kaleid (K1) and Kaleid Flux (K1 Flux) are different controllers with different firmware. The T4 Kaleid is not an Xbox-console controller, and flashing K1 firmware onto a T4 Kaleid makes it unusable and unrecoverable.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I calibrate the sticks and triggers? GameSir Kaleid

Hold View + Menu + Xbox and, while holding, plug the USB-C cable into a PC — the indicator blinks slowly white. Without touching LT, RT or either stick, press A; the indicator goes out. Then press LT and RT to maximum travel and rotate both sticks at full deflection three times, and press A; the indicator turns solid white. Unplug and replug before using. (The printed K1 manual lists this combo as View + Xbox; GameSir's web manual lists View + Menu + Xbox.)

Source: gamesir.com

How do I switch profiles and turn on hair trigger mode without software? GameSir Kaleid

Profiles: M + Y = default, M + B = profile 1, M + A = profile 2, M + X = profile 3. Hair trigger: hold M + LT or M + RT for 2 seconds; with it enabled the matching LED strip lights up when that trigger is pressed, and the setting persists across restarts. Light effects: M plus left stick left/right cycles 4 effects (saved after restart). LED brightness: M plus left stick up/down, 5 levels (not saved after restart).

Source: gamesir.com

How do I get 1000 Hz polling, and what does it cost me? GameSir Kaleid

Flash the Kaleid (K1)-specific firmware upgrader — hold LOGO + Menu while connecting the cable to a PC, open the upgrader and click Download until it shows 'Upgrade SUCCESS' — then set the polling rate to 1000 Hz in GameSir Nexus. While 1000 Hz is active the 3.5 mm earphone jack and native trigger vibration are temporarily disabled, and 1000 Hz is PC-only because the Xbox console caps polling at 250 Hz.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I fully reset the controller? GameSir Kaleid

Hold View + Menu + M until the logo button blinks (GameSir's Kaleid FAQ specifies 5 seconds). This resets the profile and all settings on the controller, and is also the documented fix for the controller not powering on or off correctly and for Nexus failing to detect it.

Source: gamesir.com

How much measured input lag does it have? GameSir Kaleid

Gamepadla measured the K1 Kaleid over cable in XInput at a 1000 Hz median polling rate: average button latency 5.32 ms and average stick latency 7.62 ms (firmware 1.49). Dropping to 500 Hz raised average stick latency to 14.21 ms and 250 Hz to 27.22 ms. No stick centering, circularity or linearity measurements have been published for this model.

Source: gamepadla.com

Which app should I use — GameSir Connect, the GameSir app, or GameSir Nexus? GameSir Super Nova

GameSir Connect on PC, or the GameSir app on mobile. GameSir Nexus does not support the Super Nova (it is for GameSir's Xbox-licensed controllers). Connect requires a wired or dongle connection in XInput mode; it cannot reach the controller over Bluetooth.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I pair the Super Nova to a Nintendo Switch? GameSir Super Nova

On the Switch home screen open Controllers, then Change Grip/Order, and leave that page open. Set the rear mode switch to Bluetooth and press Home to power on. Hold Home + Y for 2 seconds until the channel indicator flashes up and down rapidly, then wait for the connection. If it fails, hold Home + Screenshot to force pairing mode and retry.

Source: gamesir.com

What are the Bluetooth mode combos for each platform? GameSir Super Nova

With the mode switch in the Bluetooth position: Home + B for 2 seconds selects PC/other-device mode (appears as 'Wireless Controller'), Home + Y selects Switch mode, and Home + A selects Android mode (appears as 'GameSir-Nova Pro'). Home + Screenshot held 2-3 seconds forces pairing mode from any of these.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I calibrate the sticks and triggers? GameSir Super Nova

Set the triggers to long-travel mode first. Hold [-] + [+] + Home for 2 seconds to enter calibration — the Home light flashes slowly in white. With nothing touching the sticks or triggers, press A once. Then rotate both sticks in full circles 2-3 times and fully press both triggers three times, and press A to exit.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I switch between XInput, Switch, and Android/other input modes? GameSir Super Nova

Hold the [-] (View) and [+] (Menu) buttons to cycle through XInput, Switch and other-device modes. This only works in dongle and wired modes, not over Bluetooth. In XInput mode, indicator lights 2 and 4 illuminate.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I reset the controller? GameSir Super Nova

There are two resets. For a hardware force-off when the controller will not power on or off, press the recessed Reset button in the circular hole on the back with a paperclip. To reset the profile and all settings, hold Home + Menu until the Logo button blinks (GameSir's support FAQ specifies 5 seconds, until the Logo LED flashes green twice).

Source: gamesir.com

How long does the battery last, and how do I check the level? GameSir Super Nova

GameSir's estimate, provided to TechRadar on request, is about 15 hours from the 1000mAh cell; no figure is published on the product page. There is no percentage readout in the software or on the controller — the only battery indicator is the Home light flashing red when charge drops below 15%. The controller auto-powers-off after 10 minutes of inactivity.

Source: www.techradar.com

Does it work with Xbox consoles? GameSir Super Nova

No. The Super Nova supports PC, Switch, Android and iOS only. Xbox consoles require an Xbox-licensed GameSir model.

Source: www.windowscentral.com

Can I change the button layout? GameSir Super Nova

Yes, in two ways. Physically, both the middle faceplate and the ABXY buttons are detachable so you can rearrange the caps. In firmware, hold M + the south face button to swap the A-B and X-Y button values.

Source: gamesir.com

Is the T7 Pro wired or wireless? GameSir T7 Pro

The base T7 Pro (Floral, Retro Green Wired) is wired only, with a 3 m detachable USB-C cable. Wireless versions are sold as separate products: the T7 Pro Sugar Whirl (tri-mode: 2.4 GHz dongle, Bluetooth, USB-C, and officially wireless on Xbox via the receiver) and the T7 Pro Tri-Mode, which GameSir documents as 2.4G + wired on PC, wired only on Xbox, and Bluetooth for Android.

Source: gamesir.com

Which app do I need — GameSir Nexus, GameSir Connect, or the GameSir mobile app? GameSir T7 Pro

GameSir Nexus, on PC or Xbox from the Microsoft Store. GameSir's download page lists T7 Pro Floral and T7 Pro Sugar Whirl under Nexus. GameSir Connect is for PC-only models such as the G7 Pro 8K, Tarantula 8K and Cyclone 2, and the GameSir mobile app is for mobile controllers — using the wrong one is a common cause of 'controller not detected'.

Source: gamesir.com

What stick sensor technology does it use? GameSir T7 Pro

The wired T7 Pro uses GameSir Hall Effect (non-contact magnetic) sticks, which GameSir rates for up to 5 million cycles. The T7 Pro Sugar Whirl Wireless and T7 Pro Tri-Mode instead use GameSir Mag-Res TMR (tunnelling magnetoresistance) sticks, which GameSir describes as combining potentiometer-like precision and low power draw with Hall Effect durability. Neither is a potentiometer.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I switch to hair trigger mode? GameSir T7 Pro

Hold the M button together with LT or RT for about 2 seconds (GameSir's T7 Pro Wireless tutorial says 2 seconds; some GameSir pages say hold until the corresponding trigger vibrates). This is separate from the physical two-stage trigger-stop gear switch, which mechanically changes between long and short trigger pull.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I switch between saved profiles without opening software? GameSir T7 Pro

Press M + Y for configuration 1, M + B for configuration 2, M + A for configuration 3 and M + X for configuration 4 (the wireless T7 Pro documents four presets; GameSir's older T7-family pages document three custom profiles plus default).

Source: gamesir.com

Can I get 1000 Hz polling on an Xbox console? GameSir T7 Pro

No. GameSir states the Xbox console enforces a 250 Hz polling-rate limit, so the 1000 Hz option is PC-only. GameSir also advises reverting to 250 Hz if you hit headset problems.

Source: gamesir.com

Does the T7 Pro have a gyroscope? GameSir T7 Pro

The wired T7 Pro does not. GameSir's list of gyro-equipped Xbox controllers includes the T7 Pro Sugar Whirl (6-axis, PC only), G7 Pro, G7 Pro Wuchang, G7 Pro Zenless Zone Zero and Tarantula Pro Xbox — the wired T7 Pro is not on it, and Gamepadla contributors also record 'Gyroscope function: No' for the T7 Pro Floral.

Source: gamesir.com

Where is the M (Mode) button? GameSir T7 Pro

Directly underneath the D-pad.

Source: gamesir.com

Pressing the Xbox button opens the console shutdown menu instead of the guide — is the controller faulty? GameSir T7 Pro

No, this is an HDMI-CEC interaction. Open the guide, go to Profile & system > Settings > General > TV & display options > Device Control and fully disable HDMI-CEC, or make sure 'Console turns on other devices' and 'Switch TV input using controller' are not both enabled.

Source: gamesir.com

Which software do I need — GameSir Nexus or GameSir Connect? GameSir Tarantula Pro

GameSir Connect, connected via dongle or cable in XInput mode. GameSir Nexus does not recognize the multiplatform Tarantula Pro. Connect also will not see the controller if it is in DS or NS mode over Bluetooth. The mobile GameSir app can also configure it. (The separate 'Tarantula Pro for Xbox' is the opposite case — it uses GameSir Nexus.)

Source: gamesir.com

What platforms does the Tarantula Pro support? GameSir Tarantula Pro

PC, Switch, iOS, Android, and Steam. It does not support Xbox consoles — that requires the separate wired 'Tarantula Pro for Xbox' model.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I change the face button layout between Xbox and Nintendo? GameSir Tarantula Pro

GameSir's FAQ says to hold the combo for 2 seconds until the gear rotates and changes both the layout and the key values; the default is the Xbox layout. Reviewers document the combo as M + A for the Xbox layout and M + B for the Switch layout. The buttons physically rotate and change color to match.

Source: gamesir.com

How many remappable buttons are there and what can they do? GameSir Tarantula Pro

Nine: R4, L4, C1, C2, C3, C4, T1, T2, T3. Each can be mapped to a single button or a sequence of up to 12. Assignable values include A, B, X, Y, LB, RB, LT, RT, LS, RS, View, Menu, D-pad, and both sticks.

Source: gamesir.com

What do the back button latches do? GameSir Tarantula Pro

They mechanically enable or disable the L4/R4 back buttons so you can prevent unintended input when you are not using them.

Source: gamesir.com

How do I pair the Tarantula Pro to a phone over Bluetooth? GameSir Tarantula Pro

Hold the controller's combo for 2 seconds until channel indicators 1 and 5 flash, then release. Open your phone's Bluetooth list, find 'GameSir- Tarantula Pro' and connect. The channel indicator goes solid when connected.

Source: gamesir.com

Does it have a turbo/rapid-fire function? GameSir Tarantula Pro

Yes, with four modes: Slow (8Hz), Medium (12Hz), Fast (20Hz), and Off.

Source: gamesir.com

How many rumble motors does it have? GameSir Tarantula Pro

Two HD rumble motors. This differs by variant: the wired 'Tarantula Pro for Xbox' has four (one in each grip and each trigger), and the Tarantula 8K PC has none.

Source: gamesir.com
