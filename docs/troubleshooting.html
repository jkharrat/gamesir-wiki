Troubleshooting index

Every documented problem and fix across all covered controllers, in one searchable list. Each entry cites the source of its fix so you can check the original instructions.

Calibration procedures are not interchangeable between G7 models. The direction matters, so check which one applies to your controller before holding any button combination.

On the G7, G7 SE and G7 HE, calibration is entered by holding View + Menu + Xbox while inserting the USB-C cable. On the G7 Pro, it is entered by holding View + Xbox + Menu while already connected.

GameSir explicitly warns that applying the older hold-while-plugging-in procedure to a G7 Pro puts that controller into firmware update mode instead, leaving it unable to power on normally. Recovery requires a manual firmware upgrade from a Windows PC. Older guides and videos still circulate the wrong sequence.

Before you assume the sticks have failed

Most reported drift on these controllers is not sensor failure. Work through this order before contacting support, because the later steps mask the symptom rather than fix it.

1. Rule out anti-deadzone first

GameSir's documentation states that improper anti-deadzone configuration “may appear similar to stick drift”. Anti-deadzone exists to cancel out a game's own built-in deadzone by reporting slight stick movement when the stick is centered — which is indistinguishable from drift if set too high.

Set Anti-deadzone → Initial back to 0 in the GameSir app and retest. If you have ever adjusted this to fight an unresponsive game, this is the most likely cause.

2. Check whether you are in Raw (zero deadzone) mode

Several models toggle between a Raw stick trajectory with zero deadzone and a Circular trajectory. Raw is more sensitive and exposes the stick's natural center error directly to the game. If drift appeared without explanation, an accidental toggle is worth ruling out. The exact button combination varies by model — check your model page.

3. Recalibrate — with the faceplate installed

On models with swappable faceplates, GameSir specifies that calibration must be performed with the faceplate installed, because calibrating without it produces incorrect range values. This is easy to get wrong on a controller designed to have its faceplate removed.

Rotate the sticks slowly and steadily. GameSir notes that faster rotation during calibration yields higher resulting sensitivity, so a rushed calibration can leave the sticks feeling twitchy.

4. Only then add a small deadzone in software

If a small center error remains, add the smallest inner deadzone that stops the drift. Every point you add is precision you give up, which matters most in games with low native deadzones.

In Steam, a deadzone set under calibration will not apply until you enable it in the game's controller configuration: set Enable Deadzone to Configuration, then raise Deadzone Inner. Setting a value without switching this over is why people conclude Steam's deadzone slider does nothing.

If your 3.5 mm headset stopped working

Check your polling rate before troubleshooting the headset. On the G7 and G7 SE, and on the G7 HE, selecting a report rate above 250 Hz disables the controller's onboard audio entirely — no game sound and no microphone. This is by design rather than a fault, and GameSir support advises locking the rate to 250 Hz if you use a headset through the controller.

It is an easy trap to fall into, because raising the polling rate is one of the first things people do after installing the app, and the audio failure shows up later with no obvious connection to it.

All documented issues

Controller is connected and lit but registers no input, disconnects constantly, or will not power on. GameSir G7 HE
Likely causePID recognition failure between controller and host, or the controller sitting in the wrong input mode (XInput vs GIP).
FixWhile connected, hold the Xbox/Home button + M button for 3 seconds (an indicator tone should sound), then unplug and replug to restore the original PID and device recognition. If that fails, hold the Xbox/Home button + Share button for 3 seconds while connected to force-switch to XInput. Also move to a rear motherboard USB port rather than a case port or hub, and swap in a verified data-capable cable.

Source: gamesir.com

Random disconnects, failure to power on, or dead inputs traced to the installed firmware. GameSir G7 HE
Likely causeGameSir names firmware 6.33, 6.38 and 6.5.6 as known problematic versions for the G7 SE and G7 HE that cause connection-related issues.
FixCheck your version in GameSir Nexus (the 'Firmware Upgrade' arrow at top right) or Legacy GameSir Nexus ('Updates' on the controller page) and update to a current stable build. If Nexus does not offer the update, use GameSir's standalone manual upgrader for G7 HE/SE on a PC.

Microswitch face buttons or the d-pad double-click or send the wrong input; a bumper auto-clicks on its own. GameSir G7 HE
Likely causeCorrupted mapping/profile state or outdated firmware.
FixHold the Xbox button + Menu button for 5 seconds while connected to run the function reset, then update firmware to the latest version in GameSir Nexus (GameSir's page cites V6.40 as the target at time of writing), falling back to the standalone PC upgrader if Nexus does not show it.

Source: gamesir.com

3.5 mm headset produces no audio and the mic does not work. GameSir G7 HE
Likely causeReport rates above 250 Hz disable the controller's onboard audio by design; secondary causes are an incompletely inserted headset plug or an outdated firmware build.
FixGameSir support says the polling rate in GameSir Nexus on PC 'should be locked at 250hz to avoid potential problems'. Push the headset plug fully flush, confirm the mic is not muted (red LED near the bottom of the pad, or double-tap M to toggle), and on Xbox set Settings > General > Volume & audio output > Headset audio to 'Stereo uncompressed'. If it persists after a firmware update, test a different CTIA-standard headset.

Source: gamesir.com

Sticks appear to drift straight out of the box after the initial deadzone is set to 0 in GameSir Nexus. GameSir G7 HE
Likely causeNot hardware drift. Zero deadzone is a true physical zero, making the stick hypersensitive to residual movement.
FixGameSir's official answer is to set the initial deadzone to roughly 3-5 rather than 0. For genuine stick or trigger faults, run the manual Stick & Trigger Calibration and the function reset (Xbox + Menu held 5 seconds).

Source: gamesir.com

Controller becomes unresponsive or refuses to power on after a button combo was held while the cable was inserted. GameSir G7 HE
Likely causeHolding Xbox + Menu, or View + Xbox + Menu, WHILE plugging in enters DFU (firmware update) mode or manual calibration mode instead of normal operation. GameSir also warns that cable guards can make a half-seated cable feel fully inserted.
FixUnplug, reinsert the cable firmly with no buttons held until it clicks, then hold the Xbox button to power on. If still unresponsive, perform a manual firmware upgrade from a Windows PC using GameSir's standalone G7 series upgrader.

Source: gamesir.com

Back buttons are pressed accidentally during normal play. GameSir G7 HE
Likely causeThe G7 HE removed the two physical quick-latch lock switches that the G7 SE has on its back buttons, so the paddles cannot be mechanically disabled.
FixNo hardware workaround exists. Clear the mapping instead: hold M + R4 (or M + L4) for 3 seconds until the indicator blinks slowly, then tap R4 (or L4) itself to cancel its assigned value, or use a profile with the back buttons unmapped.

Source: www.windowscentral.com

Sticks appear to drift even though the controller is nearly new GameSir G7 Pro
Likely causeTMR sticks are magnetic and do not wear like potentiometers, so this is rarely mechanical. The G7 Pro applies no inner deadzone, so its natural center error is visible to games. A misconfigured anti-deadzone in Nexus can also produce it — GameSir's own documentation states improper anti-deadzone configuration 'may appear similar to stick drift'.
FixSet Anti-deadzone Initial back to 0 in Nexus, confirm you are not in Raw (zero deadzone) trajectory mode, recalibrate, then add a small Initial deadzone only if needed.
